# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free research and engineering community. Contributors must discuss people, data, methods, and results professionally; welcome constructive disagreement; respect privacy and attribution; and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or deliberately disruptive conduct.

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project events, and private project communication. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly.

## Research-specific expectations

- Critique claims and evidence, not people.
- Do not pressure maintainers to weaken provenance, privacy, licensing, reproducibility, or conflict-of-interest disclosures.
- Do not publish private data, credentials, embargoed material, or vulnerability details in a public thread.
- Preserve upstream and contributor credit, including when code is reorganized or the repository is transferred.
- Disclose material personal, academic, or commercial conflicts when they affect a review.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a GitHub Security Advisory for this repository, selecting the closest available private-reporting category and stating that the report concerns conduct. Maintainers will limit disclosure, assess conflicts, and respond proportionately. Possible actions include a private warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are a subject of the report or have a material conflict. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in a good-faith review is prohibited.
